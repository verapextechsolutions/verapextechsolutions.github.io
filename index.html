How we work

Working with a team across the world should feel easy. Here's what you can count on from day one.

  1. Discovery call

    We learn your goals, users and constraints before anything is quoted.

  2. Written scope

    A clear document listing features, timeline and price, agreed before work starts.

  3. Milestones

    Work is split into milestones, and you pay as each one is delivered.

  4. Weekly updates

    A working preview and a short progress note every week, in your time zone.

  5. Handover

    Full source code, documentation and a walkthrough, plus maintenance if you want it.
